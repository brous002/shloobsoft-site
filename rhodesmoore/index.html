A flagship $56M coastal Transit-Oriented Development in the heart of downtown Encinitas immediately adjacent to the NCTD Coaster rail station. Serosun Farms

Pioneering a new rural conservation zoning classification alongside Kane County, Serosun Farms preserves more than two-thirds of its 410-acre landscape as permanent open farmland, restored prairie, oak groves, and wetlands just outside the Chicago metro corridor.

Featured by the Urban Land Institute (ULI) in Agrihoods: Cultivating Best Practices, the community blends a 160-acre working organic market farm, premier equestrian center, event venue, and 8 miles of trails with 114 1-acre custom homesites engineered to LEED Gold and renewable geothermal/solar standards.

✓ Land Stewardship: 410 total acres • 300+ acres preserved open space & 8 miles of trails
✓ Custom Homesites: 114 1-acre lots (54 Phase 1) built to LEED Gold healthy-home standards
✓ Working Operations: 160-acre organic farm, market retail, equestrian center & event barn
✓ Agrihood Advisory: Partnered with Farmer D Consulting (Daron Joffe)
Kane County Conservation Rural Zoning Pioneer
